Future Ready Skills Academy logo - option 2 (Five Cs spark)
A light bulb with five coloured rays, one for each Future Ready Skill, and a spark inside.

Main logo (use on white or light backgrounds)
  frs-academy-logo.svg / .png              horizontal: mark + FUTURE READY SKILLS / ACADEMY / TWO RIVERS SCHOOLS NETWORK
  frs-academy-logo-stacked.svg / .png      stacked (mark above the words), for square spaces and covers

Reversed logo (use on Academy blue #005AA7 / #063F86 or other dark backgrounds)
  frs-academy-logo-reversed.svg / .png
  frs-academy-logo-stacked-reversed.svg / .png

Mark only
  frs-academy-mark.svg, frs-academy-mark-512.png                    on light backgrounds
  frs-academy-mark-reversed.svg, frs-academy-mark-reversed-512.png  on dark backgrounds

Icons
  frs-academy-app-icon.svg, -180.png (Apple home screen), -192.png, -512.png
  frs-academy-icon.svg, favicon-32.png, favicon.ico                 simplified mark for small sizes (browser tabs)

Sharing
  frs-academy-social-1200x630.jpg          image shown when the public preview link is shared

Colours: Academy blue #005AA7, deep blue #063F86, gold #EAB830, teal #00BED6, text #333333.
Rays: purple #5B2C8F, gold #EAB830, orange #E8671C, red #9B1B30, teal #00BED6 (lighter tints on dark backgrounds).
Font: Montserrat (ExtraBold, Bold, SemiBold). Words in the SVG files are outlined, so no font is needed.
Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the bulb's base. Don't stretch, recolour or add effects.
